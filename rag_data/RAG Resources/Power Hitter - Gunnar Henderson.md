
# English

## Who is Gunner Henderson?

Gunnar Henderson, born June 29, 2001, is Baltimore's present and future and a candidate to become another all-time great shortstop. He was already ranked inside the top three under-23 talents in the game for likelihood of major league success, and the reason is how unambiguous his tools are. He plays mostly shortstop, his preferred position, but he profiles cleanly at third base. His range, quickness, and athleticism are real, and his arm is strong and accurate enough on deep balls that a move to third would cost the Orioles nothing. That flexibility is also how the club solves the position problem created by Jackson Holliday, another elite prospect in the same system.

## What the rookie year established

In 2023 he hit 28 home runs with a .489 slugging percentage and a .234 isolated power, roughly 41 percent above the league average of .166. He batted .255 with a .325 on-base percentage. Solid production, but the isolated power is what identified his highest ceiling. His scouting report projected 30-30 seasons and an OPS in the .900s built on power and on-base skill. The speed supports that: 28.7 feet per second of sprint speed, top 13 percent of the league, with 10 stolen bases.

## Gunner Henderson's mechanics

Mechanically almost nothing has changed except stride length and weight transfer, both wider by about one foot width. The larger weight shift gives him a sturdier base and a wider timing window, and the swing is visibly stronger. The 21 home runs are the proof. It also fixed his real rookie problem. Against breaking balls he has gone from .237 with a .479 slugging percentage and a .330 wOBA to .333, .870, and .537. Against offspeed, from .241, .483, and .307 to .286, .612, and .573.

The irony is that the fix opened a new hole. Timing set for spin arrives slightly late on fastballs, especially in unfavorable counts, which is exactly where his breaking ball work improved most. Against fastballs he has fallen from a .250 average, a 27.2 percent whiff rate, and a 26 percent strikeout rate to .194, 29.1 percent, and 32.1 percent. His 24 percent overall strikeout rate is not the problem; Judge is at 24.4 percent. Being beaten by the pitch he sees most often is.

메커니즘상 지난해와 크게 달라진 지점은 스트라이드 폭과 체중 이동 폭뿐이며, 두 가지 모두 발 하나 정도 넓어졌습니다. 커진 체중 이동은 더 단단한 하체와 더 넓은 타이밍 싸움의 범위를 만들어 주었고, 스윙 자체도 눈에 띄게 강해졌습니다. 21홈런이 그 증거입니다. 이 변화는 그의 진짜 루키 시즌 문제도 해결했습니다. 브레이킹볼 상대 성적은 타율 .237, 장타율 0.479, wOBA 0.330에서 타율 .333, 장타율 0.870, wOBA 0.537로 올라왔습니다. 오프스피드볼 상대 성적도 타율 .241, 장타율 0.483, wOBA 0.307에서 타율 .286, 장타율 0.612, wOBA 0.573이 되었습니다.

아이러니는 이 해결책이 새로운 약점을 만들었다는 점입니다. 변화구에 맞춰진 타이밍은 패스트볼에 조금씩 늦게 반응하며, 특히 불리한 카운트에서 그렇습니다. 공교롭게도 불리한 카운트야말로 그의 변화구 대응이 가장 좋아진 구간입니다. 패스트볼 상대 성적은 타율 .250, 헛스윙률 27.2퍼센트, 삼진율 26퍼센트에서 타율 .194, 헛스윙률 29.1퍼센트, 삼진율 32.1퍼센트로 떨어졌습니다. 전체 삼진율 24퍼센트는 문제가 아닙니다. 저지도 24.4퍼센트입니다. 가장 많이 보는 공에 밀린다는 사실이 문제입니다.
